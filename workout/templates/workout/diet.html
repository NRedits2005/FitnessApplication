💧 HYDRATION

Daily Hydration & Fluid Balance

Proper hydration maintains optimal cellular function, joint lubrication, cognitive clarity, and physical endurance during exercise.

RECOMMENDED HABIT

Keep water nearby and drink regularly throughout the day.

💧

Daily Target

Aim for 2.5 to 3.5 liters of clean water daily, increasing with high sweat rates or warm climates.

⚡

Electrolyte Balance

Add fresh lemon, tender coconut water, or a tiny pinch of rock salt during prolonged training sessions.

🏋️

Pre & Post Workout

Drink 250–350 ml of water 30 minutes before exercise and replenish fluid losses steadily afterwards.

⏰

Hydration Timing

Sip water consistently throughout the day rather than chugging large volumes all at once.
